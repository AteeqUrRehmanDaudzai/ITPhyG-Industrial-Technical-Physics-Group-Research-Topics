PV End-of-Life & Circularity

Solar modules are designed for long outdoor service, but their polymer-bonded multilayer construction makes end-of-life separation difficult. The research challenge is to recover glass, aluminium, copper, silicon and silver with high purity while minimizing energy, chemicals, emissions and cost.

Research direction: compare mechanical, thermal and chemical recycling pathways; quantify material recovery, energy demand, carbon benefit and scale-up feasibility; and investigate design-for-recycling concepts for future silicon and perovskite modules.

Typical PV Module Composition

~76%Glass
~10%Plastics / polymers
~8%Aluminium
~5%Silicon

Smaller fractions include copper and silver. Their low mass fraction can still make them economically important because of their value and embodied energy.

1 · Thermal Delamination

Controlled heating or pyrolysis can remove encapsulating polymers and expose the module layers for downstream separation. A key research variable is the trade-off among temperature, residence time, energy use, emissions and material quality.

2 · Mechanical Separation

Sorting separates bulk fractions such as glass, aluminium and copper. Mechanical-only routes can reduce chemical use and cost, but recovery purity and yield may be lower for tightly bonded cell materials.

3 · High-Value Recovery

Wet-chemical processing can separate silicon and silver from cell fragments. The research priority is high recovery with low chemical intensity, closed-loop reagent use and safe industrial scale-up.

Research Portfolio

Research topics, scope and future direction

Each topic is framed as a research line rather than only a title: the scope states the problem to be investigated, while the direction identifies the next technical or scientific step.

Technical & Simulation Topics

1
Electricity Fault & Theft Detection using ML/AI
Scope: Detect non-technical losses, abnormal consumption and grid faults from smart-meter and sensor data.
Direction: Develop explainable, privacy-aware and real-time models combining deep learning, edge intelligence and cyber-physical validation.
2
Electric Energy Management & Multi-Objective Optimization
Scope: Coordinate renewable generation, storage, grid exchange and flexible demand under price and forecast variability.
Direction: Integrate forecasting with NSGA-II/MOANA-type optimization, uncertainty analysis and real-time control for cost, CO₂, peak demand and comfort objectives.
3
Smart Buildings: Energy, Comfort & Security Management
Scope: Integrate HVAC, lighting, metering, occupancy, access control and security devices within one intelligent platform.
Direction: Develop coordinated building control that jointly optimizes energy efficiency, thermal comfort, resilience and security.
4
Perovskite & Solar-Cell Technologies in Lab and COMSOL
Scope: Link device fabrication and characterization with multiphysics modeling of optical, electrical and thermal behavior.
Direction: Validate COMSOL models experimentally and optimize layer thickness, transport properties, stability, efficiency and thermal management.
5
Smart Home using Arduino, IoT & Application
Scope: Connect environmental sensors, actuators, smart loads and mobile/cloud interfaces for residential monitoring and control.
Direction: Extend the prototype toward forecasting, automated demand response, secure remote control and low-cost home energy management.
6
Datacenter / Hyperscaler Cooling
Scope: Compare air, direct-to-chip, immersion and alternative cooling architectures for high-density computing.
Direction: Optimize coolant properties, loop hydraulics, thermal resistance, pumping power, PUE and waste-heat recovery for district-energy integration.
7
Solar Panel Recycling & PV Circular Economy
Scope: Recover glass, aluminium, copper, silicon and silver from end-of-life PV modules while managing polymer encapsulation.
Direction: Compare thermal, mechanical and chemical routes; quantify recovery, emissions and economics; and investigate recyclable module and perovskite designs.

Theoretical & Review Topics

1
Security Protocols in Smart Grids / Smart Meters & Future RECs
Scope: Review encryption, authentication, key management, privacy, FDIA/DoS resilience and trusted energy transactions.
Direction: Develop REC-ready architectures using decentralized identity, secure P2P settlement and resilience during islanded operation.
2
Energy-Sharing Protocols & Techniques in SGs and RECs
Scope: Study centralized, decentralized and federated P2P trading, demand response, VPPs and interoperability.
Direction: Design fair community coordination mechanisms balancing individual profit, community welfare, grid constraints and renewable utilization.
3
Social Acceptance & Bureaucratic Effects on RECs
Scope: Examine licensing, grid connection, regulatory inconsistency, trust, ownership, inclusivity and participation barriers.
Direction: Build comparative indicators and policy recommendations for faster, fairer and more inclusive REC deployment.
4
Evolution from Traditional Grids to Smart Grids & RECs
Scope: Trace the shift from centralized one-way systems toward active, IoT-enabled, software-defined and community-based networks.
Direction: Map the role of AMI, PMUs, edge computing, IoT, distributed intelligence and community flexibility in future grid architectures.
5
Perovskite & Solar-Cell Technology Evolution
Scope: Review silicon, thin-film and emerging PV generations, tandem architectures, BIPV and stability challenges.
Direction: Connect efficiency progress with durability, thermal behavior, manufacturability, recyclability and building integration.
6
Advanced Datacenter Cooling: Air to Beyond Cooling
Scope: Review traditional air cooling, direct-to-chip, immersion, subsea concepts, orbital computing and heat reuse.
Direction: Compare technologies through thermal efficiency, water/energy use, reliability, lifecycle impact and integration with district heating.
7
Trombe–Michel Wall Review
Scope: Study passive solar heating, natural convection, thermal mass, glazing, selective coatings, PCM and PV-integrated variants.
Direction: Develop climate-specific design guidance using experiments and numerical modeling for heating/cooling performance and thermal comfort.
8
PV End-of-Life, Recycling & Design for Circularity
Scope: Review material composition, delamination, sorting, chemical recovery, recycled-material quality and current scale-up barriers.
Direction: Evaluate design-for-disassembly, recyclable encapsulants, glass-based module designs and future perovskite recycling pathways.
