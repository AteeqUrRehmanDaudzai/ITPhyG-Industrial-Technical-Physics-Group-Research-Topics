Renewable Energy Communities

The human, regulatory and technical layers of the energy transition

REC performance is not determined by technology alone. Social acceptance, inclusivity, administrative procedures, grid connection and trustworthy energy-sharing mechanisms strongly influence deployment.

Social & administrative bottlenecks

01
Administrative inertia
Licensing, grid connection and legal entity formation can slow deployment.
02
Regulatory inconsistency
Different national implementations create uneven REC conditions across Europe.
03
Trust, ownership & NIMBYism
Participation depends on local trust, perceived fairness and community ownership.
04
Energy poverty & inclusivity
REC design should enable participation by low-income and vulnerable households.

Secure community energy exchange

P2PCentralized, decentralized and federated trading architectures.
Game TheoryNash-equilibrium concepts for fair participant incentives.
ProtocolsOpenADR 2.0 and IEEE 2030.5 interoperability concepts.
VPPCommunity-scale aggregation and flexibility coordination.
Research focus: balance individual prosumer value with community well-being, resilience and equitable access.
Advanced Photovoltaics

From material science to COMSOL-driven solar-cell optimization

Research spans the evolution from conventional silicon and thin-film technologies to third-generation perovskite devices, tandem cells, BIPV and multiphysics modeling.

1G

Silicon PV

Mature crystalline technology with high bankability and strong commercial deployment.

2G

Thin-Film PV

Reduced material use, lighter modules and alternative semiconductor architectures.

3G

Emerging PV

Perovskites, tandem cells, flexible devices and semi-transparent BIPV concepts.

Physical laboratory path

1
Cell fabrication & layer preparation
2
Electrical / optical characterization
3
I–V curves and experimental data

COMSOL modeling path

1
ABX3 material and device model
2
Charge transport & optical simulation
3
Layer thickness / stability optimization
Experimental data validate the virtual model; validated simulations guide the next fabrication cycle.

Core Principle

Passive solar heating using greenhouse effect, thermal mass storage and natural convection.

Modern Variants

Classic vented/unvented configurations, PCM integration and PV–Trombe wall concepts.

Performance

Thermal efficiency, time lag, decrement factor, glazing, selective coatings and climate adaptation.

Thermal Management

Cooling the next generation of AI and hyperscale datacenters

Higher compute density is pushing datacenter cooling from traditional air systems toward direct-to-chip, immersion and heat-reuse architectures where thermal performance and pumping energy must be optimized together.

Cooling ConceptCore IdeaBest FitResearch Focus
Traditional AirCRAC/CRAH, raised floors, hot/cold aislesLegacy / lower densityAirflow losses and hot spots
Direct-to-ChipLiquid cold plates remove heat near processorsAI / high-density racksLoop design, fluid properties, reliability
ImmersionServers immersed in dielectric liquidVery high heat densityViscosity, compatibility, pumping energy
Subsea / ExtremeUse environmental conditions for thermal controlSpecial deploymentsReliability, access and heat rejection
Heat ReuseRecover waste heat for buildings / district heatingEnergy-system integrationCommunity-level thermal coupling
DI WaterHigh heat capacity reference coolant
GlycolsFreeze & corrosion protection mixtures
